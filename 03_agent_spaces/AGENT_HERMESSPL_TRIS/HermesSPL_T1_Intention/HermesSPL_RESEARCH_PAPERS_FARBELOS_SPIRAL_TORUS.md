# HermesSPL Research Papers — f-belos / arbelos / spiral / torus

Status: research seed, not canon. Use this as the first reading list for the farbelos/fCircle app and HermesSPL geometry lane.

## Research lanes

### Lane G1 — arbelos / f-belos mathematical core

The strongest immediate source is Oller-Marcén's **The f-belos**, because it formalizes a general family where one curve `f` generates the upper arc and two scaled/translated similar copies generate the lower arcs.[1] This maps well to your thought that the same form can recur across person/body/shadow/universe levels.

Sondow's **The parbelos** is the direct parabolic analogue of the arbelos and gives a bridge from semicircle geometry into similar-arc geometry.[2]

Boas's **Reflections on the Arbelos** is the best historical/mathematical orientation note: it covers classical arbelos properties, Pappus chains, Apollonian packing links, and Mohr's-circle/arbelos connection.[3]

Różański, Samulewicz, Szweda, and Wituła's **Variations on the arbelos** extends the family into generalized arcs, ellarbelos, and 3D-ellarbelos; it explicitly discusses engineering relevance such as static moments and structural stability/durability.[4]

### Lane G2 — stress / projection / body-to-plane analogies

**Mohr's arbelos** connects the arbelos with stress-state representation and constrained extrema; Boas also notes that Mohr diagrams for 3D stress can produce an arbelos-like range with cusp abscissae tied to eigenvalues.[3][5]

This is important for your shadow observation because it gives a grounded math/engineering bridge: a higher-dimensional state or tensor can project to a 2D region/diagram with arbelos structure. That does **not** prove the metaphysical claim, but it gives a serious analogy lane to test.

### Lane G3 — 3D / rotation / torus candidates

`Variations on the arbelos` defines a 3D-ellarbelos as the upper half of a solid obtained by rotating an ellarbelos around the x-axis, enclosed by a plane and three tangent ellipsoids of revolution.[4]

This is the first formal bridge to build in the app before jumping to torus claims:

1. 2D arbelos/f-belos
2. rotate/revolve into 3D arbelosoid/ellarbelos
3. inspect whether torus/fCircle behavior appears under rotation, projection, or recurrence
4. only then map to SPL metaphors

### Lane G4 — kundalini/chakra resemblance

A recent PubMed-indexed paper on kundalini explicitly frames itself as hypothesis-generating, not proof: it says its purpose is not to validate metaphysical claims, but to examine possible correspondences between phenomenological descriptions and measurable physiological processes.[6]

Use that standard for our work: chakra/kundalini resemblance can enter the farbelos app as a symbolic/phenomenological comparison lane, while physiology/physics claims stay marked as speculative until evidence supports them.

## Working hypothesis boundaries

- **Canon-safe:** f-belos/arbelos/parbelos/ellarbelos are real mathematical objects with published sources.
- **Research-safe:** shadow projection, 90° body-ground relation, and tensor/stress projection can be explored as analogies and visual models.
- **Speculation-safe:** levitation, magnetic frequency reversal, spacecraft, or universal causal claims must stay in speculation lane until separately tested.
